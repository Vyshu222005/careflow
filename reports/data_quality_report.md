## Issues found

- **Duplicate events:** 7 rows repeated the same case, activity and timestamp (repeated lab results). The first was kept.
- **Broken order:** 55 cases do not start with `ER Registration`; other events are timestamped before the patient was registered, so the pathway cannot be trusted. These cases were removed.
- **Events after discharge:** 12 events in 6 cases (late lab results or admissions after a `Release`). Kept and flagged. A `Return ER` after discharge is valid and was not counted.
- **Timestamps:** parsed as UTC. No missing or unparsable timestamps were found.
- **Sort order:** events are sorted by case and time. Events with identical timestamps keep their original order.
- **Case attributes:** Age, diagnostic flags and SIRS criteria were recorded on one event only. They are now copied to every event of the case. Cases with no `Diagnose` are labelled `Unknown`.
- **Measurements:** `CRP`, `LacticAcid` and `Leucocytes` stay empty when not measured on that event.
